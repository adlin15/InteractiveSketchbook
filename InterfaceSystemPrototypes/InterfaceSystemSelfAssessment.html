For this project's self assessment, I believe I did better than last project, considering the amount of work hours we got was much more generous and beneficial for progressing in Figma. Figma was something I used before, but was only to make a personal logo for a project a few semesters ago. It was a totally new experience to build a webpage dedicated to putting our work in while making versions for desktop and mobile. If I were to score myself, considering from last time how harsh I graded myself, I think I deserve a 98 out of 100 score, as I worked pretty hard and made lots of changes as the weeks went by. I managed to structurally cohere each page to match the requirements for the project, and went above and beyond and made the desktop version a slidebar, combining 4 different pages into one, which was apparently something unique that we were allowed to do. I studied from the apple website, on how it is structured with the buttons and side bars, trying to mimic or reflect those principles with my project, and I think I managed to capture at least more than 70% of those in my Interface System. I also adjusted the sizes to perfectly match with the iphone 16, as the mobile version had a camera that was big enough to cover the top, and shifted the title of the sketchbook down to match the camera position, including the bottom of the phone where it is curved, making the detail of the buttons stand out more if it was not pressed towards the center as the phone has a smooth corner. These minor details to using mobile phone versions made adjustments to a quality of detail that I wouldn't have noticed if I were to just focus on the desktop version, as everyone can just see everything on a flat rectangular screen. I also heavily focused on the specific hierarchy, making everything clear with titles, different font sizes, and making sure people can tell what is a description and what is something that can be clicked on with boxes. The navigation was a complex part of this project, as with the mobile version, I was inspired by another student to put arrow keys on the bottom of the mobile version so you could navigate to different sections, all while also having the left side arrow bring you back to the home page as a clear way to head back to the start. The desktop version was much different, with an idea I had by adding a fast scroll which drags the viewer straight to each respective title that leads to the projects by clicking on the name. This was a genius part in my book, as I had a scrollable sketchbook desktop so adding this made me genuinely proud, although the only part that made me slightly mad was the fact it goes under the title and below the line which I put out as a clear indicator of a new section. Some iterations I had that majorly differed was from week 3 to now. I made some dramatic changes: I added the description to the mobile version, and it made everything extra long and descriptive to read, added direct links when clicking on the projects so you can preview and see my projects in correspondence to the name, and added the writing sections to the project. As for the last part, there were some things I had some difficulty with, which I forgot to screenshot as I made changes midway while sorting everything out as weeks went by so I will only briefly mention what went wrong. One thing I was disappointed with was trying to add previews of the projects by trying to figure out how to put the display of each project as a preview, which didn't work since I didn't know how to use the FigJam. I also had no idea what to fill some empty spaces so I just left them there if I had any other bright ideas, which I forgot. Overall this was a fun project and I was proud to work on this one.
